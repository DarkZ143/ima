Chronicle of Service

History & Milestones

1928

Foundation in Calcutta

Established during the 5th All India Medical Conference to unite Indian medical practitioners under one national banner.

1946 & 1956

Global Medical Leadership

Became a founding member of the World Medical Association (WMA) and later joined CMAAO to shape international health ethics.

1974

IMA House, New Delhi

Inauguration of the National Headquarters at Indraprastha Estate, anchoring IMA’s academic wings and policy cells.

2025–2026

Digital & Clinical Vanguard

Advancing AMR stewardship, 24/7 legal defense, and unified digital registries for over 400,000 doctors nationwide.

IMA Headquarters

The largest representative voluntary organization of Doctors of Modern Scientific System of Medicine in the world, safeguarding healthcare and clinician welfare across India.
